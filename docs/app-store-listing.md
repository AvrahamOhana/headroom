## Description (4000)
Headroom turns your iPhone or iPad into a professional guitar rig built around Neural Amp Modeler — the same capture technology behind today's flagship modelers, with thousands of free amp and pedal captures available from the community.

BUILD YOUR RIG BY DRAGGING
• Two complete signal paths (A ∥ B), each with its own level and pan into a stereo output
• Add any block as many times as you like — two delays, an EQ before and after the amp, two amps
• Drag tiles to reorder or move them between paths; a block keeps its settings wherever it goes
• Rotary knobs with double-tap reset, LED status on every block, a glance readout of each block's key setting

THE AMP
• Loads any .nam capture (WaveNet A1/A2, LSTM, custom)
• Level-matches every capture from its own loudness metadata so quiet captures don't turn into hiss
• Second capture slot for pedal captures in front of the amp
• Cab impulse responses (.wav / .aiff), including TONE3000 cabs

EFFECTS THAT SOUND RIGHT
• Noise gate with hysteresis, hold and range (mute or gentle expander)
• Soft-knee compressor with gain-reduction meter
• Anti-aliased, oversampled drive; circuit-modeled stompbox overdrives and fuzz
• Wah — expression pedal or auto
• Tape-style delay with tempo sync and tap tempo
• Room, plate, spring and hall reverbs, plus convolution reverb from your own IRs
• Chorus, flanger, tremolo, 3-band EQ, clean boost
• Stereo widen stage and a phrase looper

TONE3000 BUILT IN
• Sign in with your TONE3000 account and browse Trending, Favorites, your own captures and downloads
• Search with sort and filters, favorite tones, one-tap download of captures and cab IRs
• Captures you download stay yours, on your device

MADE FOR THE STAGE
• Presets with a setlist manager; a large Live view for the floor
• MIDI: Program Change to presets, learn any CC or note, expression pedal, momentary or latching footswitches, Bank Select, MIDI clock in
• MIDI out: Program Change on preset load, a per-preset send list to control your other gear, CC feedback to your controller, clock out
• Bluetooth MIDI pedals pair right inside the app
• Low-latency engine with a latency/stability picker

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Completely free: no purchase, no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no tracking, and no account required (TONE3000 login is optional). The source code is open under the GPL.

Requires an audio interface with an instrument input (iRig, Scarlett, or similar). Headroom is not affiliated with Neural Amp Modeler, TONE3000, or any amplifier or pedal manufacturer.

## App Review Information — Notes (paste)
Headroom processes live guitar audio in real time and is designed for an audio interface with an
instrument input (iRig, Focusrite Scarlett, etc.). For evaluation without an interface, playing any
instrument or voice into the device microphone works: tap the power button at the top right if the
engine is not already running, then play. The TONE3000 browser requires a TONE3000 account and is
optional — every other feature works without signing in. No login is required to use the app.
