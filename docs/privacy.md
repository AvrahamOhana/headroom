# Headroom — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 September 2026_

Headroom is a guitar amplifier and effects app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Avraham Ohana.

## The short version

Headroom does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. Everything you play, record or configure stays on your device.

## Audio

Headroom processes the audio from your audio interface / microphone in real time to produce your guitar tone. Audio is processed live on the device only. It is never recorded, uploaded or shared unless you explicitly use the looper (which keeps the loop in memory only until you clear it or close the app).

## Data stored on your device

Presets, MIDI mappings, downloaded captures and impulse responses are stored locally in the app's own storage on your device and are not sent anywhere. You can delete them from within the app or by deleting the app.

## TONE3000 (optional)

If you choose to connect a TONE3000 account, Headroom signs you in through TONE3000's own login page (OAuth) and then uses your account to browse and download captures you are entitled to. Headroom stores the resulting access token on your device only, and sends it only to `tone3000.com`. Headroom never sees your TONE3000 password and shares nothing about you with TONE3000 beyond the requests needed to browse and download. TONE3000's handling of your account is governed by their own privacy policy. You can disconnect at any time from the TONE3000 screen or Settings; this deletes the token from your device.

## MIDI and Bluetooth

Headroom talks to MIDI controllers (USB, network or Bluetooth) that you connect. It only exchanges MIDI messages with them; no data is collected.

## Children

Headroom is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
